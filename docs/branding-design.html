01 · Position

Not another meditation library.

Sama should own a narrower, clearer promise: breathing practice that adapts to you and makes your progress visible—without putting the basics behind a paywall.

“Breathe by your numbers.”

This line connects the two strongest differentiators: any four-phase breathing pattern you choose, and health signals shown alongside your practice.

AccessibleEssential practices stay free, simple, and welcoming.
AdaptableFrom classic box breathing to a personal 4–4–6–4 rhythm.
Evidence-awareShow patterns in wellness data without making medical claims.
02 · Name

A name built around balance.

Short enough for an app icon, broad enough to grow beyond box breathing, and connected to pranayama without sounding clinical.

Product name
Sama

From the Sanskrit root associated with evenness and balance. It gives the brand cultural grounding while staying easy to say and remember.

Pronounced: SAH-mahStore title: Sama BreathDescriptor: Pranayama & insights

Why it works

  • Feels calm without drifting into spa clichés.
  • Works for beginners and experienced practitioners.
  • Leaves room for sleep, recovery, and wearable insights later.
  • Pairs naturally with direct copy: “Find your Sama.”
Before launch: run trademark, App Store, Play Store, domain, and social-handle clearance. This is a creative recommendation—not legal clearance.

04 · In the app

The brand should make data feel humane.

Warm surfaces for practice. Clear, restrained charts for progress. Coral appears only when attention is needed—never as decoration.

App icon

One recognizable breath form, four measured phases, strong contrast at small sizes.

4464

Custom patterns

Numbers are tactile and prominent. Phase color remains semantic—sky inhale, saffron hold, coral exhale, mist recovery—while duration is communicated by the number.

Progress, not scores

Use “trends” and “patterns,” never grades. Wellness data offers context, not judgment.

05 · Voice

06 · Launch kit

One message everywhere.

Lead with freedom and flexibility. Let wearable insights support the story rather than overwhelm the first impression.

Your breath. Your rhythm.

Practice box breathing or create your own four-phase pattern. Sama keeps the essentials free and helps you see your consistency alongside supported health signals.
