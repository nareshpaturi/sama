# Naming exploration

Research date: September 23, 2026 (two rounds). Status: **decided: Viram** (September 23, 2026). The docs, mocks, and app configuration now use Viram; trademark clearance and registering viram.app are the remaining prelaunch steps.

## Why rename

“Sama Breath” is already live on the App Store: rarePixel LLC, Health & Fitness, launched February 2026, version 2.3 (September 12, 2026), bundle ID `com.samabreath.sama`. It is a breathwork and daily-reflection app that calls itself “Sama” in its copy ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sama-breath/id6758898071)). App Store names must be unique, and another “Sama” breathwork app in the same category creates confusion and intellectual-property risk. Google Play also lists another meditation app with “Sama” in its name ([Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.darmac.sama)). samabreath.com (registered December 2025, “Coming Soon”) appears to belong to rarePixel; **samabreath.app belongs to us.**

**What stays:** the two-breaths mark, “Steady breath. Steady mind.”, Pine & Paper, and all product work. **What changes:** the wordmark, name gloss, store name, app name, bundle ID, and share-link domain.

## Criteria

- Rooted in Sanskrit or yoga, faithful to the practice, and easy for English speakers to say and spell.
- A meaning that fits the brand: evenness, steadiness, rest, or the pause between breaths.
- Not a “Prana-” cliché (Prana Breath owns that space) and not a sacred term (Om, deity names).
- Works in Hindi for the India-heavy audience.
- Clear of breathing, meditation, yoga, and wellness apps on the App Store (US and India) and Google Play.
- A usable `.app` domain for share links.

## Method

Candidates were checked with the App Store Search API (US and India), Google Play search, and the `.app` (Google Registry RDAP) and `.com` (Verisign RDAP) registries, then web-searched for wellness brands with the same name. **This is not a trademark clearance.** A professional search (USPTO, India Trade Marks Registry, EUIPO) is still needed before committing.

## Recommendation

**Viram.**

- It completes the brand system. The mark's coral point is already defined as the pause between breaths, and “Viram” names that pause. The voice already says “Take your time” and “A little space, made.”
- It's short, easy to say (vee-RAHM), easy to spell, and it renders well in Newsreader.
- It means something ordinary and true in Hindi (a pause; the full stop) without being sacred or clichéd.
- It's clear in both stores, and **viram.app is unregistered**, which suits share links (`viram.app/r/…`).

**Runner-up: Nischal.** It has the deepest link to the tradition and the tagline, but the pronunciation and given-name risks are higher.

Name gloss if Viram is chosen: **“Viram (विराम): a pause, a place to rest.”**

**Round 2 outcome:** no candidate beats Viram on the combination of brand fit, easy English pronunciation, clean store results, and a free own-name `.app`. The best alternatives are **Nishwas** (both domains free, a direct breath meaning, but the “sigh” sense) and **Thehrav** (for an India-first identity). **Recommendation unchanged: Viram.**
