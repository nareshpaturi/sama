01 / Position

Make room for a small ritual.

For someone who wants a breathing break without setting up a wellness routine, Sama is a free, offline companion with a ready-to-start practice and adjustable timing.

One promise

A simple breathing practice, on your terms.

Lead with agency and ease. Custom timing supports that promise. Local history quietly remembers the practice. Optional Health logging is a convenience after completion.

Primary brand line: Your breath. Your rhythm.
Descriptor: Guided breathing, at your pace.
01
Welcoming

One clear next action. Familiar words. Safety and cue choices are easy to understand.

02
Personal

Begin with a familiar pattern; adjust it when you want. The default is useful without personalization questions.

03
Steady

Predictable timing, quiet surfaces, and permission to pause. The product never evaluates how well someone breathed.

Retired lead line: “Breathe by your numbers.” It overemphasized measurement and implied a biometric product. Wearable insights remain a future capability, outside the launch promise.

02 / Identity

One name. One familiar mark.

Sama in the app and in conversation. Sama Breath in stores and first mentions. The name is intended to evoke evenness and balance; pronunciation guidance is “SAH-mah.”

Sama

The breath mark

Retain the existing pine field, sky breath form, measured strokes, and coral point. Use this same construction in the onboarding welcome, brand lockup, and icon specimen. The mark stays still during practice; the circular guide carries the motion.

Small-size specimens

Keep clear space of at least one quarter of the mark width. Use the full mark at 29 px and above; below that, use the wordmark or a simple breath silhouette. Keep the store source square and opaque; the platform supplies its icon mask.

Do not stretch, rotate, add glow, or substitute a leaf, lotus, or a different breathing symbol. Use a single-color silhouette when reproduction cannot preserve the interior details. Name and cultural-language clearance remain prelaunch tasks; this board records the design direction.

05 / Voice

Warm. Specific. Unhurried.

Use a short invitation, then say exactly what happens. Acknowledge the action someone took without promising a feeling or judging their consistency.

Welcome“Your breath. Your rhythm.”
“Simple guided breathing, ready offline.”

Avoid“Transform your nervous system.”
“Your path to perfect calm.”

Pause / end“Take your time.”
“Your time so far will stay in History.”

Avoid“Don’t give up.”
“You’ll lose your progress.”

Completion“A little space, made.”
“Saved on this device.”

Avoid“You’re calmer now.”
“You crushed your goal.”

Optional Health“Add sessions to Apple Health?”
“Your practice is saved here either way.”

Avoid“Unlock your health insights.”
“Health access granted” before a confirmed write result.

Exact phase vocabulary: Inhale → Hold after inhale → Exhale → Rest. Explain Rest as the pause after exhaling. Keep “Begin,” “Pause,” “Resume,” “End session,” “Done,” and “Breathe again” consistent. Discuss pranayama respectfully in background material; do not imply the timer teaches a full tradition.
06 / Launch kit

A promise the first release can keep.

Show the core experience in store imagery. Health logging appears only if it is in the released build. Voice, wearable charts, streaks, and reminders stay out of launch copy.
